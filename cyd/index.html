PlaneCast CYD

Turns an ESP32 "Cheap Yellow Display" into a little box that keeps the plane map on your Nest Hub.

This browser can't flash devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page needs to be opened over https.
  1. Plug the CYD into a computer with a USB data cable and open this page in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Press Install on the CYD, pick the port that appears (often "USB Serial" or "CH340"), and confirm. It takes about a minute.
  3. Plug the CYD into any USB charger near the Hub. Its screen will say Wi-Fi setup.
  4. On your phone, join the Wi-Fi called PlaneCast-Setup. A page opens (or go to 192.168.4.1). Pick your home Wi-Fi, type its password and press Save.
  5. The CYD finds the Hub and puts the map up. From then on it recasts within a few seconds whenever the Hub drops back to the clock or photos.
On the CYD screen: Cast now puts the map up straight away, even over something else. Pause stops it recasting when you want the Hub for something else; tap Resume after. It never interrupts music or video you've cast to the Hub.

Change Wi-Fi or settings: hold the BOOT button on the back for 3 seconds and repeat step 4.

If the install button can't find the CYD: try another cable (some only charge), or hold BOOT while pressing RST, then try again.
